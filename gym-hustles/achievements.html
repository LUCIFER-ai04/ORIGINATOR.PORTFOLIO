The Journey

10 Years of Hustle

Every milestone was earned with sweat, sacrifice, and an unwavering belief that this work genuinely changes people's lives.

2015

The Beginning — First Steps on the Gym Floor

Started training seriously at a small local gym in Theni, Tamil Nadu. Consumed every book, video, and resource I could find on training science. The obsession that would define the next decade had begun.

2016

First Personal Training Certification (CPT)

Earned the first formal personal training certification. Immediately began applying the theory with volunteer clients — friends, family, and fellow gym members. The real education had started.

2017

Launched Personal Coaching Practice — First 30 Clients

Officially launched as an independent personal trainer in Theni. Within the first year, built a roster of 30 paying clients. Word-of-mouth results started building a reputation for no-nonsense, results-focused coaching.

2019

Sports Nutrition Specialist Certification

Completed advanced Sports Nutrition Specialist certification. Combined training science with strategic nutritional programming — dramatically improving client results and differentiating the coaching service.

2020

100+ Client Transformations Milestone

Reached a major milestone: over 100 documented client transformations. Photos, testimonials, and changed lives were the proof that the system worked — even through the challenges of the pandemic period.

2021

Strength & Conditioning Coach — Advanced Certification

Expanded expertise with a dedicated Strength and Conditioning certification, adding elite performance programming capabilities for competitive athletes and serious recreational lifters alike.

2022

Became Lead Coach at Premier Theni Gym

Appointed as Lead Coach at one of Theni's most respected training facilities. Overseeing client programmes, mentoring junior trainers, and setting the standard for coaching excellence across the floor.

2023

300+ Transformations & Expanded Online Coaching

Surpassed 300 transformations and launched structured online coaching to reach clients beyond Theni. Remote programming, video form checks, and WhatsApp accountability check-ins extended the reach significantly.

2024

400+ Clients — Recognised as Top Trainer in Theni District

Recognised locally as a leading fitness professional in the Theni district. Over 400 clients served. Building a coaching system trusted by everyday people, working professionals, and competitive athletes.

2025

500+ Transformations — 10 Years in the Iron Game

A full decade. 500+ documented transformations. 200+ active monthly clients. Six certifications. One mission: to keep building physiques and changing lives — one hustle at a time. The journey is nowhere near finished.
